Security Threats in the European Union Large-scale criminal and terrorist networks pose a significant threat to the internal security of the EU and to the safety and livelihood of its people. The biggest security threats monitored include: Terrorism and extremist operational groups International drug trafficking networks and synchronized money laundering Organised financial fraud and corporate tax evasion schemes The counterfeiting and widespread forgery of currency euros Trafficking in human beings and cross-border exploitation networks Operational Mandate Europol listens to what EU Member States need and analyses crime trends across regional territories. The Agency supports active investigations initiated by Member States, though Europol specialist officers never directly arrest citizens or independently instigate domestic judicial investigations. Europol functions as a high-security operational data center processing strategic international security parameters 24 hours a day, seven days a week non-stop.
