About Europol

Helping make Europe safer

Our Mission

Headquartered in The Hague, the Netherlands, Europol’s mission is to support its Member States in preventing and combating all forms of serious international and organised crime, cybercrime and terrorism. Europol also works with many non-EU partner states and international organisations.

Security Threats in the European Union

  • Terrorism and extremist operational groups
  • International drug trafficking networks and synchronized money laundering
  • Organised financial fraud and corporate tax evasion schemes
  • The counterfeiting and widespread forgery of currency euros
  • Trafficking in human beings and cross-border exploitation networks

Operational Mandate

Europol listens to what EU Member States need and analyses crime trends across regional territories. The Agency supports active investigations initiated by Member States, though Europol specialist officers never directly arrest citizens or independently instigate domestic judicial investigations.

Europol functions as a high-security operational data center processing strategic international security parameters 24 hours a day, seven days a week non-stop.
